# Driver Safety & Incident Workflows

## 1. Principles
- An incident report is an objective operational record, not automated proof of driver guilt.
- Every incident undergoes a structured review workflow with documented investigation findings.

## 2. Categories & Severities
- **Categories:** `ACCIDENT`, `TRAFFIC_VIOLATION`, `VEHICLE_DAMAGE`, `SAFETY_COMPLAINT`, `DELIVERY_INCIDENT`, `OTHER`.
- **Severities:** `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`.

## 3. Investigation States
1. `OPEN`: Filed by dispatcher, safety officer, or driver. Generates an instant high-priority notification if severity is `HIGH` or `CRITICAL`.
2. `UNDER_REVIEW`: Assigned investigator reviews evidence, dashcam/telemetry logs, and police reports.
3. `RESOLVED`: Corrective action, coaching module completion, or claims notes recorded.
4. `CLOSED`: Final audit sign-off.
